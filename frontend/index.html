Our Integrated Project Workflow

How we manage commercial spaces from pre-construction layout concepts to on-site assembly handover.

01.

Estimating & BoQ

Our estimation engineering team analyzes drafts to deliver complete, itemized Bills of Quantities (BoQs) and material calculations.

02.

Technical Detailing

Industrial designers develop full CAD shop drawings and 3D architectural renders to resolve joins and load balances.

03.

In-House Production

Custom woodwork, robotic welding, laser cutting, acrylic shaping, and LED diagnostics occur in our central Umm Al Quwain factory.

04.

Logistics Coordinates

Completed fixtures are securely crated in compliance with ISPM-15 guidelines and dispatched via our GCC logistics networks.

05.

On-site Assembly

In-house MEP and fitout site teams handle partition glazing, drywall installation, ceiling grids, and local civil defense checks.

High-Precision Production Capacity

800 Retail operates under Al Ameen Industries LLC. Our main manufacturing base, located in Umm Al Quwain, UAE, spans over 300,000 square feet, facilitating high-volume production for GCC retail rollouts.

By integrating design, joinery, metal fabrication, acrylic laser cutting, digital printing, and LED diagnostic lines in-house, we eliminate intermediate delays and retain strict quality control over material tolerances.

Engineering & Prototyping Hub

Concept Engineering & Detailing

While our central manufacturing operations are based in Umm Al Quwain, 800 Retail coordinates client consultations and prototyping approvals from our corporate trading showroom in Deira, Dubai (conveniently located near Abu Hail Metro Station).

Our in-house design studio is staffed by experienced retail architects and technical draftsmen who transform initial brand blueprints into production-ready CAD files. We build custom prototype mockups and organize physical sample boards—featuring wood veneer stains, PVD metal finishes, and LED color temperatures—to guarantee zero variance before starting production run volumes.
