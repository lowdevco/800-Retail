The 5S Methodology

Rooted in lean manufacturing principles, the 5S methodology enhances productivity by systematically organizing the workplace. Below is the operational framework we apply across all our facilities.

5S SystemDefinitionAction
SeiriSortEliminate what is not needed
SeitonSet In OrderOrganise what remains
SeisoShineClean the workplace
SeiketsuStandardiseEstablish standards
ShitsukeSustainMaintain and improve
