Who We Are

Discover the legacy of manufacturing quality and integrated operations behind Al Ameen Industries LLC.

Established 2004

Building Trust Through Production Quality

For nearly three decades, 800 Retail (operating under the Al Ameen Industries LLC banner) has built a legacy of manufacturing excellence. Starting as a localized joinery shop, we have evolved into a major B2B retail partner operating a 300,000 sq. ft. consolidated manufacturing environment spanning wood, metal, acrylics, and intelligent retail displays.

Our strength lies in direct vertical control. By maintaining in-house robotic metal fabrication, certified DuPont solid surface processing, and next-generation LED diagnostic labs in Umm Al Quwain, we eliminate external supplier bottlenecks. This translates to absolute precision, short lead times, and structural compliance for hypermarkets and boutique outlets alike.

Our Progress

Timeline of Engineering Milestones

2004

Founding under Al Ameen Group

Established custom woodworking facilities to fabricate custom store fixtures, cash counters, and joinery fittings for early commercial boutiques in Dubai and UAQ.

2004

Inception of 800 Retail Brand

Formally launched the 800 Retail brand under Al Ameen Industries LLC to consolidate high-volume retail fixture manufacturing, CNC wood cutting, and metal fabrication under one specialized division.

2012

UAQ Manufacturing Facility Consolidation

Expanded and consolidated all fabrication operations into a single massive 300,000 sq. ft. plant in Umm Al Quwain, UAE. Added specialized laser cutting and automated electrostatic powder coating lines.

2018

LED, Print Integration & Saudi Expansion

Integrated next-generation LED screen assembly and UV printing divisions into UAQ production. Formally opened Saudi Arabia operations hubs in Riyadh and Jeddah.

2026

5S Lean Operations & Global Sourcing

Full adoption of the 5S Lean manufacturing systems at our Umm Al Quwain facility, resulting in a 15% reduction in lead times. Operation of procurement networks in Foshan, China.
