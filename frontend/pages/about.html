Designing. Building. Inspiring.

At Frame&Co., we believe in creating spaces that combine beauty, functionality, and trust.

Our Impact
Welcome to Frame&Co.
Our mission is to turn inspired ideas into spaces that endure—through design that unites creativity and precision.
At Frame&Co., we believe remarkable spaces don’t start with blueprints—they start with vision. Our role is to translate that vision into structures that elevate daily life, combining aesthetics, function, and sustainability. We partner with homeowners, developers, and businesses to shape environments that stand the test of time—built with care, intention, and innovation.
We go beyond traditional design and construction. Our team leads every phase—from the first sketch to final inspection—aligning craftsmanship with modern technology and responsible materials. Whether it’s a home renovation or a large-scale commercial build, we create spaces that reflect purpose and perform beautifully.
What drives us is simple: we want to be the consulting team you’d hire if your reputation depended on it. Because often, it does.
We believe great strategy is useless without traction—and great execution fails without clarity. Nexa was built to do both. We’re not interested in handing you decks. We’re here to think with you, build with you, and get things across the line. That’s our work. That’s our standard.
Sarah Collins
Founder & Principal Architect

Our Journey So Far

From concept to construction excellence, here’s how Frame&Co. has evolved—one milestone at a time
2015
The Vision Begins
Born from a passion for purposeful design and sustainable building, Frame&Co. was founded to bridge the gap between creativity and craftsmanship in modern architecture.
2017
First Foundations Built
We completed our first residential and small-scale commercial projects, setting a standard for precision, communication, and client-centered design.
2019
Expanding Horizons
Our portfolio grew to include large-scale builds and urban renovation projects. We adopted advanced 3D modeling tools and green building practices to elevate quality and sustainability.
2022
Recognition & Growth
Frame&Co. earned industry recognition for design excellence and environmental responsibility. Our team expanded, partnering with developers and design firms across the region.
2024
Building for the Future
We launched our e-commerce design packages and online consultation services—making professional architectural expertise accessible to anyone, anywhere.
