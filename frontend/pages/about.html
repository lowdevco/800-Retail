Who We Are

Discover the legacy of manufacturing quality and integrated operations behind Al Ameen Industries LLC.

The Origin
of Our Journey

Al Ameen Group, the largest fixture manufacturing company in the Middle East, strives to create a culture of value in the retail sector. We are committed to delivering functional, innovative, and revolutionary solutions. We focus on state-of-the-art design concepts that cater to the diverse needs of our clients.

Our inspiration comes from distinct innovation, forward-thinking, technical expertise, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. 800 Retail Solutions is the brand that manufactures custom designed retail furniture and consumer goods. We collaborate closely with clients to deliver the highest-quality products, supported by our in-house design team, technicians, and cutting-edge equipment. We proudly represent home-grown brands in the United Arab Emirates with the "Made in UAE" unified national mark a symbol of formality and trust for locally manufactured solutions.

Take on Tomorrow

A vision for long-term value creation

We embrace retail through creative thinking, innovative design, and the manufacture of unique products. 800 Retail Solutions focuses on practical, prefab, modular, and contemporary solutions. We adopt a continuous, stepwise approach to innovation.

Our mission is to build a strong local organization and to cross-share global best practices. We design localized products for the emerging-market consumer and ensure a seamless omnichannel experience. 'Focus on the known' is the strategy we implement in our work ethic. Regarding products, we maintain a balanced perspective on design aesthetics and functionality.

Our design strategy conveys our clients' stories, visions, and brand values. Our mission is to deliver forward looking solutions that challenge conventional thinking to create inspiring, innovative, and sustainable spaces.

Next-Gen LED Integration

800 Retail Solutions LED reserves its capacity to innovate, invest, and acquire in the next generations of LED technology. Combined with an entrepreneurial and forward-looking corporate culture, 800 Retail Solutions LED will continue to lead the industry by providing crisper, small-pitch screens that are cost-effective, quick to install, easy to maintain, and deliver the best possible experience to our clients and their end-users.

Studies have shown that carefully planned use of lighting in retail environments leads to increased sales. It helps establish a store's image, attract customers inside, focus their attention, and make products stand out.

Good color, contrast, and balance between surfaces are important, but lighting should not be over-designed. Energy-efficient retail lighting emphasizes good color, contrast, and balance between illuminated surfaces. To achieve this, designing and calculating the right lux levels is extremely important.

Global Presence

Manufacturing and procurement hubs scaling across borders.

Since 1997
UAE

Headquarters & Manufacturing

Al Ameen Group of Companies is a leading industrial force based in the United Arab Emirates, with a strong operational presence across the UAE. Operating from a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility spanning over 300,000 square feet and powered by a skilled workforce of over 450 employees, Al Ameen excels in delivering high-quality solutions across retail and commercial fixtures, LED display panels, architectural lightboxes, and custom fabrication.

With a proven track record of completing more than 9500+ store counters in over 25 countries, the group has established a strong global footprint marked by precision, reliability, and innovation. Al Ameen also features a dedicated experience center in Dubai, offering clients a first-hand look at our craftsmanship, innovation, and capabilities. Known for its financial stability and commitment to excellence, Al Ameen stands as a trusted partner for international brands seeking end-to-end manufacturing and fit-out solutions in the region and beyond.

Since 2022
KSA

Regional Expansion

Al Ameen Industries has firmly established its presence in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with a growing footprint and expanding capabilities. Our dedicated manufacturing facility in Riyadh spans over 40,000 square feet and is supported by a skilled team of 50+ employees, with plans in place to expand operations in response to increasing market demand. Strategically positioned to serve the Kingdom end-to-end, Al Ameen delivers customized solutions in retail fixtures, LED display systems, architectural lightboxes, and bespoke fabrication.

We proudly operate experience centers in both Riyadh and Jeddah, offering clients the opportunity to engage with our design innovation, product quality, and manufacturing capabilities firsthand. With comprehensive service coverage across Saudi Arabia, we provide efficient and localized project execution, backed by the reliability and standards of the Al Ameen Group. Committed to consistency, performance, and long-term partnerships.

Since 2023
CHINA

Procurement & Sourcing Hub

Al Ameen - Global Retail Solution continues to expand its international capabilities with a dedicated procurement and sourcing hub in Foshan, China. This facility features a 6,000+ square foot experience and procurement center that serves as a crucial platform for material selection, product sampling, and collaboration with vendors across Asia. It enables our teams and clients to access an extensive range of finishes, components, and innovations aligned with global quality standards and project requirements.

To further strengthen our manufacturing footprint, a new 40,000+ square foot furniture production facility is currently under development in Foshan. This expansion is aimed at supporting high-volume, precision-driven furniture manufacturing for international retail and commercial markets. Al Ameen's China operations play a strategic role in enabling agile sourcing, cost-efficient production, and robust supply chain integration, ensuring timely and scalable support for projects worldwide.
