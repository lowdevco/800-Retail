B2B Case Studies

Explore the precise engineering challenges and custom fabrication solutions delivered for our partners.

The Challenge

Fabricating high-load metal columns that could wrap mall concrete supports while housing flush-mount P2.5 digital LED panel grids. The structure required custom tolerances of less than 0.2mm to prevent visible gaps.

The Solution

Our metal and LED divisions collaborated to design a segmented 3.5mm thick mild steel skeletal framework. The pieces were pre-cut via fiber lasers in our DIC plant, welded with robotic support, and fitted with specialized heat dissipation cooling slots.

Scope Executed

  • 300+ Custom clothing displays manufactured
  • Concealed 24V LED strip woodwork integration
  • 1,200 sq. ft. digital column casings
  • Turnkey night-shift execution & compliance signoff

The Challenge

Deploying consistent commercial layouts across multiple cities in KSA under tight retail occupancy dates, requiring simultaneous customs clearing and on-site assembly.

The Solution

Adopting pre-fabricated modular joinery templates. Fixtures were finished, wired, and packed into custom crates at the Dubai Industrial City factory, then shipped to KSA nodes for quick assembly by local teams.

Scope Executed

  • Modular double-sided retail gondolas
  • CNC cut acrylic back-lit branding boards
  • Jeddah & Riyadh localized site coordination
  • Turnkey MEP and lighting approvals

The Challenge

Creating acoustic corporate zoning using double-glazed structural glass walls while integrating timber ceiling panels that conceal primary air ducts.

The Solution

Designed timber acoustic ceiling baffles suspended via sound-damping mounts. Smart-glass partitions were installed, changing from clear to opaque electronically.

Scope Executed

  • 24 Executive offices completed
  • Suspended acoustic baffles
  • MEP air balancing and HVAC rerouting
  • Seamless microcement flooring installation
