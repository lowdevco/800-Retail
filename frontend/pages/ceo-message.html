“For us, the significance of a project lies not in its size, but in its meaning and the impact it has on our clients.”

Welcome to 800 Retail. Since our inception under the Al Ameen Group banner in 2004, our philosophy has been rooted in building a strong local organization that cross-shares global best practices while delivering functional, modular, and sustainable retail environments.

We are deeply grateful for the trust our clients place in us, and we focus on turning your vision into reality—creating inspiring retail spaces that provide comfort, pride, and joy.

Operating from our central 300,000 sq. ft. manufacturing plant in Umm Al Quwain, UAE, we ensure our homegrown solutions represent excellence through our “Made in UAE” unified mark. We believe in prefab, modular, and contemporary structures that optimize layout efficiency and consumer flow.

The true measure of our success is the smile on a client’s face upon completion of a project. That feeling is more valuable to us than any milestone or award. We look forward to executing your retail rollout concepts with absolute precision.

Muhammed Manaf
