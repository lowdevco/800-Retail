Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the most common questions we get from clients

Services & Capabilities

What types of projects do you specialize in?
We focus on residential, commercial, and cultural developments—ranging from new builds and large-scale renovations to architectural restorations and interior fit-outs.
Do you provide both design and construction?
Yes. Our integrated design-build approach allows us to manage both the creative and technical sides of each project, ensuring seamless collaboration from concept to completion
Can you work with existing architectural plans?
Absolutely. We frequently collaborate with clients who already have approved drawings, offering construction expertise, technical refinement, and value engineering.
Do you take on small projects or only large-scale builds?
While we’re known for our larger developments, we also take pride in thoughtfully crafted small-scale projects that require precision and creativity.

Process & Collaboration

What does the design and construction process look like?
Our process includes three key phases: discovery and planning, design and documentation, and construction and delivery. We keep clients informed every step of the way through transparent communication and progress updates.
How long does a typical project take?
Timelines vary by scope and complexity. A small renovation may take a few months, while larger cultural or commercial builds can extend to a year or more. A detailed schedule is provided during project planning.
How do you ensure projects stay within budget?
We combine accurate cost estimation, proactive planning, and value-driven design decisions to prevent overruns. Every financial decision is reviewed collaboratively with the client before execution
Can I make changes during the project?
Yes—our flexible workflow allows for adjustments when needed. We evaluate design or material changes for cost and impact before implementation to maintain quality and efficiency.

Sustainability & Innovation

Do you incorporate sustainable or green building practices?
Yes. Sustainability is at the core of our approach. We integrate eco-friendly materials, energy-efficient systems, and waste-reduction strategies into every project whenever possible.
What certifications or standards do you follow?
‍Our designs align with international sustainability standards such as LEED and BREEAM, and we collaborate with environmental consultants to meet or exceed regulatory requirements.
Can you design a building to be net-zero energy?
‍Absolutely. Through smart orientation, passive solar design, renewable energy systems, and efficient materials, we can create buildings that produce as much energy as they consume.
Do sustainable features increase project costs?
‍Some features may have higher initial costs, but they often result in long-term savings through energy efficiency, durability, and reduced maintenance.

Project Management & Delivery

Who will manage my project day-to-day?
Each project is overseen by a dedicated project manager who coordinates between the design team, contractors, and client—ensuring smooth communication and timely execution.
How do you handle quality control?
‍We maintain rigorous site supervision, regular inspections, and close collaboration with certified contractors and engineers to uphold the highest construction standards.
What happens if there are delays?
‍We proactively identify risks during planning and communicate any necessary adjustments immediately. Our structured scheduling minimizes disruptions while maintaining quality.
Do you offer post-construction support?
‍Yes. Once your project is complete, we conduct a final quality review and provide ongoing support to address any questions, warranty items, or maintenance guidance.
