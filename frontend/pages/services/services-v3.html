General
Services
Process
What types of projects do you typically work on?
We work on residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects—ranging from custom homes and renovations to office spaces, retail environments, and large-scale developments.wer
Where are you located and do you take projects outside your area?
We are based locally but regularly take on projects in surrounding regions. For select projects, we also collaborate remotely or travel as needed.
What makes your firm different?
We combine architectural creativity with construction expertise, ensuring designs are not only beautiful but also practical, cost-efficient, and buildable.
Do you offer free consultations?
Yes, we offer an initial consultation to understand your vision, goals, and budget before outlining next steps.
What industries or clients do you specialize in?
We work with homeowners, developers, and businesses across sectors including residential, retail, hospitality, and office environments.
What services do you provide?
We offer end-to-end services including architectural design, planning, construction documentation, project management, and construction oversight.
Can you handle both design and construction?
Yes, we provide a seamless design-build approach, managing everything from concept through completion for a more efficient process.
Do you work on renovations or only new builds?
We handle both. Whether it’s a full renovation, extension, or a brand-new project, we tailor our approach to your needs.
Do you help with permits and approvals?
Absolutely. We manage planning permissions, building permits, and coordinate with local authorities to ensure compliance.
Can you work with my existing contractor or team?
Yes, we’re happy to collaborate with your chosen contractors or consultants to ensure smooth project delivery.
How does the project process work?
Our process typically includes discovery, concept design, detailed planning, approvals, and construction. We guide you through each stage step-by-step.
How long does a typical project take?
Timelines vary depending on project size and complexity, but we provide a clear schedule early on and keep you updated throughout.
How involved will I be in the process?
As involved as you’d like. We maintain regular communication and key decision checkpoints while handling the technical details for you.
How do you manage budgets and costs?
We prioritize transparency and cost control from the start, providing detailed estimates and working closely with contractors to stay within budget.
What happens once construction begins?
We oversee the build to ensure everything aligns with the design, quality standards, and timeline—keeping you informed at every stage.
