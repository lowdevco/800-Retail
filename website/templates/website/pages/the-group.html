The Group

Design. Engineer. Manufacture. Deliver.

From concept to completion, we create complete retail environments built around your brand.

Retail solutions that shape tomorrow’s storefronts

At Al Ameen Group of Companies, we don’t just manufacture retail fixtures — we build immersive brand experiences.

Founded in 2004 in the heart of the UAE, Al Ameen has transformed from a niche fabricator in Dubai’s Deira district into a cross-border production powerhouse, with state-of-the-art facilities spanning over 350,000 sq. ft. across United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia.

Al Ameen Group proudly announces its new division in Gurgaon, India, marking another milestone in our global expansion. This strategic addition strengthens our manufacturing capabilities and extends our retail solutions expertise into the Indian market.

A growing global presence, driven by innovation and built for the future.

End-to-end retail environments crafted with precision and consistency.

From the first spark of concept design to the last bolt installed on-site, AAGC delivers end-to-end retail environments for global and regional brands — all crafted in-house. Our ability to engineer, fabricate, and install full-scale retail units makes us a true one-stop solution partner.

  • 12,500+ projects delivered across 25+ countries
  • Teams for fixtures, LED displays & Lighting & Ceiling solution and Signages
  • Serving the region’s leading fashion, sports & luxury brands
  • 100% in-house LED display & EU-Standard lighting solutions
  • Backed by over 350 skilled professionals & decades of expertise

Our Evolution

The journey, philosophy, and technology that define 800 Retail Solutions.

01

The Origin

Al Ameen Group, the largest fixture manufacturing company in the Middle East, strives to create a culture of value in the retail sector. We are committed to delivering functional, innovative, and revolutionary solutions.

We collaborate closely with clients to deliver the highest-quality products, proudly representing home-grown brands with the "Made in UAE" national mark—a symbol of trust for locally manufactured solutions.

02

Philosophy

Take on Tomorrow

We embrace retail through creative thinking, innovative design, and the manufacture of unique products. Our strategy conveys our clients' stories, visions, and brand values.

Our mission is to deliver forward-looking solutions that challenge conventional thinking to create inspiring, innovative, and sustainable spaces.

03

Vision

We are building the world's largest retail solutions production centre, combining innovation, technology, and world-class manufacturing. Through a forward-looking culture, we create smarter, scalable solutions that help brands deliver better retail experiences.

We are also developing the next generation of retail leaders through knowledge, collaboration, and hands-on experience. By investing in people and innovation, we aim to shape the future of retail and create lasting value for our clients and partners.

For us, retail is more than creating attractive spaces. It is about understanding how people interact with brands and translating that understanding into purposeful environments that are functional, engaging and built to last.

From the initial concept through design, manufacturing and installation, we bring every stage together under one integrated approach, ensuring consistency, quality and attention to detail from beginning to end.

Our focus is simple: delivering commercially effective, visually distinctive and purpose-built retail environments from concept to completion.

methodology

Japanese 5S System Methodology

We apply the 5S System across all divisions to promote operational excellence, safety, and workplace efficiency. Rooted in lean manufacturing principles, the 5S methodology enhances productivity by systematically organizing the workplace. Through the steps each division is empowered to eliminate waste, streamline processes, and uphold high standards. This disciplined approach not only improves day-to-day performance but also fosters a culture of continuous improvement across the organization.

5S System Definition Action

Seiton Set In Order Organise what remains
Seiso Shine Clean the workplace
Seiketsu Standardise Establish standards
Shitsuke Sustain Maintain and improve
