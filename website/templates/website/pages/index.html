Our Network

16 Specialized Divisions

A unified ecosystem of specialized departments driving operational excellence through the 5S methodology.

Management Division

Implements Seiri (Sort) to eliminate redundant processes, and Seiton (Set in Order) to structure workflows. Seiketsu (Standardise) ensures consistent governance, while Shitsuke (Sustain) promotes long-term organizational discipline and continuous improvement.

Infrastructure & Facilities Division

Applies Seiri to declutter storage areas, Seiton to organize logistics tools and utilities, Seiso (Shine) to maintain cleanliness across workshops, and Seiketsu to uphold safety protocols. Shitsuke ensures operational excellence across facilities.

Business Development & Strategic Partnerships

Uses Seiri to focus on viable partnerships, Seiton for managing client records and leads, and Seiketsu to standardize business proposal formats. Shitsuke drives sustained growth through disciplined client engagement.

Client Consulting & Advisory Services

Applies Seiri to tailor insights to client goals, Seiton to organize tools and deliverables, and Seiketsu to standardize consulting frameworks. Shitsuke supports consistent, high-impact advisory outcomes.

Marketing & Digital Communications

Uses Seiri to filter outdated content, Seiton to organize media assets and campaigns, Seiketsu for consistent brand tone and visuals, and Shitsuke to sustain digital presence and metrics.

Design & Engineering Solutions

Implements Seiri to remove design redundancies, Seiton to structure CAD libraries and material specs, Seiketsu to apply engineering standards, and Shitsuke to promote disciplined innovation cycles.

Research & Development (R&D)

Applies Seiri to focus only on relevant prototypes, Seiton to organize testing environments, Seiso to ensure precision conditions, Seiketsu for experimental frameworks, and Shitsuke for continuous idea validation and innovation.

Quality Control (QC)

A core strength of Al Ameen, QC uses Seiri to eliminate non-conforming parts early, Seiton to structure inspection areas, Seiso for spotless inspection labs, Seiketsu to define testing parameters, and Shitsuke to uphold a quality-first culture.

Machinery & Core Production Division

Applies Seiri to remove unused tools, Seiton to organize workstations, Seiso to maintain clean machines, Seiketsu to document best practices, and Shitsuke to reinforce habits through training.

Fabrication Technologies Division

Utilizes Seiri to streamline raw materials, Seiton to mark workstations and tool zones, Seiso for spotless welding and powder coating stations, Seiketsu to document finishing specs, and Shitsuke to ensure continuous quality and efficiency.

Joinery & Finishing Works Division

Applies Seiri to optimize raw wood inventory, Seiton to arrange tools and materials, Seiso to ensure dust-free, clean environments, Seiketsu for uniform finish quality, and Shitsuke for craftsmanship standards.

Installation & Execution Services

Uses Seiri to prepare only needed tools for site work, Seiton to sequence installation steps, Seiso to maintain clean, hazard-free job sites, Seiketsu for installation SOPs, and Shitsuke to ensure on-time, consistent execution.

Digital Media & LED Display Solutions

Applies Seiri to manage components and content, Seiton to label and arrange control systems, Seiso to ensure clear displays, Seiketsu to standardize LED calibration, and Shitsuke for long-term media system performance.

Stretch Ceiling & Lighting Division

Implements Seiri to remove outdated modules, Seiton for logical lighting layouts, Seiso for clean finishes and environments, Seiketsu to define install techniques, and Shitsuke to ensure client-ready presentations every time.

Printing & Signage

Follows Seiri to declutter print queues, Seiton for logical storage of media and inks, Seiso for clean printer areas, Seiketsu to define color standards and templates, and Shitsuke for continuous output quality.

After-Sales Services

Delivers ongoing technical support and AMS with Seiri applied to simplify client service records, Seiton to organize manuals and service kits, Seiso to maintain demo equipment, Seiketsu for ticket handling protocols, and Shitsuke to ensure responsive, reliable support.
methodology

Japanese 5S System Methodology

We apply the 5S System across all divisions to promote operational excellence, safety, and workplace efficiency. Rooted in lean manufacturing principles, the 5S methodology enhances productivity by systematically organizing the workplace. Through the steps each division is empowered to eliminate waste, streamline processes, and uphold high standards. This disciplined approach not only improves day-to-day performance but also fosters a culture of continuous improvement across the organization.

Seiketsu Standardise Establish standards

5S System Definition Action
Seiri Sort Eliminate what is not needed
Seiton Set In Order Organise what remains
Seiso Shine Clean the workplace
Seiketsu Standardise Establish standards
Shitsuke Sustain Maintain and improve
