End-to-End Manufacturing

Founded in 2004 in the heart of the UAE, 800Retail has evolved from a niche fabricator in Dubai’s Deira district into a cross-border production powerhouse, with state-of-the-art facilities spanning over 300,000 sq. ft. across the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia.

From the first spark of concept design to the final on-site installation, 800Retail offers end-to-end retail environment solutions for global and regional brands all crafted entirely in-house. Our integrated approach to engineering, fabrication, and installation makes us a true one-stop retail solution partner.
