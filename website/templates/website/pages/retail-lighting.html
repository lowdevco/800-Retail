Explore the complete ecosystem of tracks, joints, lighting, and lightboxes designed for seamless integration.

D'Track Main Rails

Smart connectors that keep your lighting tracks perfectly aligned and powered clean, seamless, and reliable.

Linear Joint

Connects two track rails end-to-end, forming a seamless, straight path without angles. Essential for extending tracks smoothly.

L-Joint

Joins two track rails at a right angle. Commonly used when tracks need to change direction, such as turning a corner.

T-Joint

Forms a "T" shape to branch out a track into multiple directions from a single point, enabling complex junctions.

+ Joint (Cross)

Four track rails meet at a central point. Allows for highly intricate and interconnected track layouts.

End Cap

Provides a clean and finished look to the end of the rail, concealing exposed ends and preventing dust accumulation.

End Cap with Power Hole

A clean finish to the end of the rail while allowing easy access for electrical connections.

Hanging Suspension Wire

Utilized to support track rails, ensuring stability and alignment in elevated or overhead track systems.
